## How to Check

1. Read the task list and collect every checkbox, checked and unchecked.
2. For each task marked complete `[x]`, find the code that does what the task
   says and cite `file:line`. Search the whole repository, not just the diff -
   the work may have landed in an earlier commit on this branch.
3. A task is unsupported when nothing implements it, when only a stub or a
   TODO implements it, or when the implementation covers a different thing than
   the task describes.

Do not edit the task list. Reporting is the whole job; the executor decides
whether to implement the work or uncheck the box.

## What to Report

For each unsupported completed task:
- Task: its text and its line in the task list
- Evidence: the search you ran and what it found, or the `file:line` that only
  partially implements it
- Severity: critical when the task is a core deliverable of the change, major
  otherwise
- Fix: implement the task, or uncheck it and say why

Also report, as minor findings:
- Tasks still unchecked whose work is clearly present and complete on the branch
- Tasks whose wording no longer matches what the change actually does

Skip tasks that only a human can carry out - manual verification, deployment,
credential setup. Note them once as out of scope instead of reporting each.
